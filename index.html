Ilmify Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Overview

Ilmify is an Islamic learning app for iPhone and iPad with Prayer Studio, a Wudu guide, Arabic learning and speaking practice, Explore Islam lessons, a Qur’an reader and read-along, Dhikr & Awrad, widgets and optional Support purchases. Learning works without an Ilmify account or a developer-operated cloud progress service. An optional recitation donation, “Improve our system for everyone”, is off unless you turn it on; it is described in its own section.

Who Is Responsible

Ilmify is provided by Jason Omar Turkmani, who is responsible for the app and for this privacy policy. For privacy questions, requests or feedback, email Jason.omar.turkmani@gmail.com.

Data Stored On Your Device

Ilmify stores information locally so you can continue between sessions. This can include:

  • onboarding completion, language and interface preferences;
  • prayer-reader and Wudu positions;
  • Arabic lesson progress, practice responses, self-review choices and listening and recording-practice metadata;
  • Explore Islam lesson progress;
  • Qur’an reading positions, bookmarks and coloured ayah highlights;
  • Dhikr counters, saved and favourite readings, and reminder settings;
  • reader preferences, such as text size, translation and transliteration visibility, appearance and movement pace;
  • explicit in-app completion totals used by My Activity; and
  • local purchase and entitlement information supplied through Apple services.

Completion totals describe actions completed inside Ilmify. They do not establish whether a person performed worship outside the app. Answers to the first-launch questions about your background and interests are used to suggest a starting place and are not saved as a religious-affiliation profile.

The app uses local preferences and files in its private app storage. It does not automatically upload your learning history to the developer. Apple-managed device backups are separate and depend on your device and backup settings.

Optional Microphone Practice

Microphone access is optional. Ilmify requests permission when you choose to record an Arabic speaking exercise or start Qur’an read-along, not simply when you open the app. You can decline permission or revoke it in your device’s Settings; features that need the microphone will then be unavailable.

Arabic speaking practice lets you listen to an example, record yourself and replay your recording for self-review. It records a temporary audio file in the app’s private on-device cache, with a maximum recording length of 60 seconds. These recordings are not uploaded or sent to a remote speech-recognition or pronunciation-grading service. Practice uses your own review rather than an automated pronunciation score.

The app attempts to delete the current recording when you start a fresh attempt, leave the practice screen or the practice is interrupted, including when the app goes into the background. A crash, unexpected termination or file-cleanup failure can leave a recording in the cache. The operating system may clear cached files; Ilmify’s all-learning-data erase control also includes the recording cache. Recordings are not included in Ilmify learning-backup exports.

Qur’an Read-Along And Local Session Records

Qur’an read-along processes microphone audio on your device with a recognition model supplied with the app, to follow your place in the text. Live audio and recognised text are used in memory during processing. The normal read-along feature does not save an audio recording or a raw transcript to disk, and does not send your recitation to the developer or a remote recognition service, unless you turn on the optional recitation donation described in the next section.

The app does retain local read-along session and diagnostic records. These can include a session identifier, dates and times, duration, the passage selected, surah, ayah and word positions, processing and stopping results, hashes of recognition output, model information, and technical audio-input, signal and route details. These records can indicate which passages you recited even though they contain no recording or raw transcript.

These records stay in the app’s local storage and are not automatically sent to the developer. They have no automatic age-based expiry in the current app. Ilmify’s all-learning-data erase control includes these records and the saved session reference. Session and diagnostic records are not included in Ilmify learning-backup exports.

Improve Our System For Everyone (Optional Recitation Donation)

“Improve our system for everyone” is an optional feature in Data & Privacy, in Ilmify versions that offer it. It is off by default. Ilmify collects nothing through it unless you open it, confirm that you are 16 or older and agree on its consent screen. Not taking part changes nothing else in Ilmify.

What is collected

  • Audio: recordings of the Qur’an recitations you make with the microphone in read-along (including Memorize mode) after agreeing, as 16 kHz mono audio. Recordings shorter than two seconds are not kept, and at most ten minutes of each session is kept.
  • Related details: the Qur’an ayah and page shown, the calendar day of the recording (not the time), its length, the app version, whether the app came from TestFlight or the App Store, the device type (iPhone or iPad), the major iOS version and the consent version.
  • A random donor code: created for this purpose. It is not your name, Apple ID, email address, contacts or location. Apple’s CloudKit also attaches its own pseudonymous, app-specific user reference to each upload. Ilmify cannot see your Apple ID from it and does not keep it when downloading recordings.

A voice recording is personal data, and donated recordings are grouped under your donor code. We therefore treat them as linked to you, and Ilmify’s App Store privacy details say so. We do not use them to identify who is speaking.

How it is sent and stored

Recordings are sent only over Wi-Fi or Ethernet, never over mobile data, Personal Hotspot or a Low Data Mode connection, and only while you are signed in to iCloud. They go to Ilmify’s storage in the public database of Apple’s CloudKit, which Apple operates as Ilmify’s hosting provider. Its access rules stop other users from reading them. Apple may process this data in other countries, including the United States.

A copy of each recording stays on your device until you delete it. These copies are kept in Ilmify’s private app storage with file protection, are excluded from device backups and are not included in Ilmify learning-backup exports. So that you can withdraw later, even after reinstalling the app, your donor code and a list of your uploads are also kept in your own private iCloud database, which Ilmify cannot read, and the donor code is kept in your device’s keychain, which iCloud Keychain syncs to your other devices if you use it. Both are deleted when you withdraw.

The app limits how much it collects: about 100 MB or 60 recordings a day, about 500 MB in total for each installation, and at most about 500 MB or 1,000 recordings kept on the device. When a limit is reached, the app stops keeping new recordings for donation and says so on the donation screen.

How it is used

Only to test and improve how Ilmify follows and checks recitation, for example how well it notices missed, wrong or extra words and vowel marks. This includes training and evaluating Ilmify’s own recognition models. Recordings are processed on computers controlled by Ilmify’s developer. They are not sold, not shared with other companies, not used for advertising or tracking, not sent to third-party AI or speech-recognition services, and not used to identify speakers.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Because a Qur’an recitation can reveal religious belief, it is explicit consent to the processing of sensitive data (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR; Art. 6(7) Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection). You can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before.

How long it is kept

There is no fixed time limit. Recordings and their details are kept until you delete them or withdraw, or until Ilmify no longer needs them to test and improve its recitation features, whichever comes first. Then they are deleted from CloudKit and from Ilmify’s computers. Withdrawal notices (a donor code and recording names, no audio) are kept as long as Ilmify keeps any donated recordings, so that withdrawn recordings stay deleted, for example if an older copy is restored from a backup.

Age

Donating is only for people aged 16 or older. The consent screen asks you to confirm this, and nothing is sent without that confirmation. If we learn that someone younger donated, we delete those recordings. A parent or guardian can ask for this by email, with the donor code shown in the app.

Pausing, deleting and withdrawing

  • Pause: turn off donating on the donation screen. Nothing new is kept or sent until you turn it on again.
  • Delete a single recording: it is deleted from your device at once. If it was already sent, its copy is deleted from CloudKit the next time the app can reach iCloud, and it is never sent again.
  • “Withdraw and delete everything”: deletes all your donation recordings from your device at once and withdraws your consent. As soon as the device is online and signed in to iCloud, the app deletes your recordings from CloudKit and leaves a withdrawal notice for Ilmify. Recordings made before you withdraw are never sent afterwards.
  • Erasing all learning data in Data & Privacy also withdraws the donation in the same way.
  • After deleting the app or on a new device: install Ilmify, sign in to the same iCloud account and tap “Withdraw earlier donations”.
  • By email: write to Jason.omar.turkmani@gmail.com with the donor code shown in the app after your first donation.

Copies Ilmify has already downloaded are deleted at its next data update, within 30 days. This includes recordings sent from another iCloud account, which the app itself cannot delete. Models that were already trained are not retrained, but your recordings are left out of all further training and evaluation.

Learning Backups And Deletion

You can export a learning-backup file and choose a saved file to restore through Data & Privacy. Supported backup data includes learning progress, prayer and Wudu positions, Arabic course progress, the current Explore Islam course’s progress, Dhikr counters, completion totals, saved and favourite readings, and Qur’an bookmarks and highlights. Backups exclude microphone audio, read-along session and diagnostic records, presentation preferences, purchase entitlements and credentials. Restoring a backup replaces the supported learning data after you confirm.

You choose where to save an exported file using the system file picker. A service you choose, such as iCloud Drive, may store or sync that file under its own terms. Ilmify does not automatically send backup files to the developer or operate its own cloud backup service.

The Data & Privacy erase control removes supported learning records, completion totals, the Arabic recording cache, read-along session records and any recordings kept for the optional recitation donation, and it withdraws that donation as described above. Presentation preferences are managed separately. Erasing data in Ilmify does not delete backup files you saved elsewhere; remove those copies through the file location or service you chose. We cannot retrieve learning data that is stored only on your device or in your own backup files.

Reminders And Widgets

Dhikr reminders are optional local notifications. Ilmify asks for notification permission when you enable a reminder. It does not send your reminder times to a developer-operated server or register a remote push token for these reminders.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show Qur’an excerpts bundled with the app. The excerpt and language are selected locally from your choices or a daily rotation. The widgets do not read microphone recordings or completion totals, and do not send your learning data to the developer.

Optional Support Purchases

Optional Support purchases are processed by Apple. Ilmify receives product and verified transaction information to complete purchases, restore eligible purchases and determine purchase benefits or subscription status. Payment-card details are not provided to Ilmify. Learning does not depend on contributing.

Apple manages billing and subscription cancellation. Removing Ilmify or erasing learning data does not cancel a subscription; use Apple’s subscription settings.

Analytics, Feedback And TestFlight

Apart from the optional recitation donation described above, the current app does not use developer-operated analytics or advertising services and does not track users across other companies’ apps or websites. Local completion totals and read-along diagnostics are not automatically transmitted to the developer.

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares information with the developer. Feedback you submit may include comments, screenshots, device information and your name or email, depending on how you joined the beta and what you submit.

We use this information to investigate problems and improve Ilmify. We do not use it for advertising or share TestFlight testing information with third parties. Details about Apple's collection and retention are available in TestFlight & Privacy.

If you contact us or choose to send a report through your email or sharing app, we receive what you send, including your email address and any sender name your mail service supplies. The app does not automatically send that report. Include only the information needed for your request. We use correspondence to respond, provide support and investigate the issue.

We retain support correspondence and any developer-held beta feedback only as long as needed to handle the request, investigate the issue, or meet applicable legal obligations. Apple controls retention within its own services.

External Content Links And This Website

Qur’an text, translations, tafsir and other learning material supplied with the app are read locally. If you choose to open a source, book, privacy or other external link, your browser contacts that website. The website may receive technical information such as your IP address and the page requested, and its privacy policy applies. Choosing an email, file-storage or sharing service also subjects what you send to that service’s terms. These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

The public privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising or tracking scripts to this page. GitHub may process technical information when you visit it, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Religious Learning Content

Ilmify is a learning tool and does not provide personal religious rulings, live fatwa or emergency guidance. Source references are presented where the app provides them.
