Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers Ilmify for iPhone and iPad, including Prayer Studio, the Wudu guide, Arabic learning and speaking practice, Dhikr and Awrad, widgets, local learning backups, optional Support purchases, and TestFlight testing.

Who is responsible

Ilmify is provided by Jason Omar Turkmani. For privacy questions, requests, or app feedback, email Jason.omar.turkmani@gmail.com.

Learning data stays on your device

Ilmify stores onboarding completion, prayer and Wudu reading positions, Arabic lesson progress and practice responses, Dhikr counters, saved and favorite readings, reminder settings, language choices, and app preferences locally so you can continue between sessions. Arabic progress includes lesson attempts, self-review choices, and listening and recording-practice metadata; the audio recordings themselves are handled separately below. You do not need an Ilmify account to use the learning features.

The app also keeps local totals when you explicitly complete a prayer guide, Wudu guide, daily Dhikr round, or mark a supplication as read. These totals describe actions in the app; they do not establish whether a person performed worship outside the app. They contain no activity timestamps or user or device identifiers, and existing reading positions are not converted into past completions.

Ilmify does not upload your learning progress to a developer-operated server or provide its own cloud progress sync. Device backups managed by Apple are separate and depend on your device and backup settings.

Completion totals remain on your device. The app does not provide a shared activity dashboard or automatically share these totals.

You can erase app learning data and completion totals using the Data & Privacy controls in the app. Local data otherwise remains on the device while it is retained by the app. We cannot retrieve learning progress or completion totals that are stored only on your device.

Dhikr reminders are optional local notifications. The app asks for notification permission when you enable a reminder; it does not register a remote push token or send your reminder times to a developer-operated server.

Optional microphone practice

Arabic speaking exercises let you listen to an Arabic system voice, record your own voice, and replay it for self-review. Ilmify requests microphone permission when you choose Record after listening to the model. It does not request microphone access when you first open the app. You can decline the request or revoke microphone access in your device's Settings; recording then remains unavailable.

Your voice is recorded into a temporary audio file in Ilmify's private on-device cache, with a maximum recording length of 60 seconds. The app does not upload these recordings or send them to a remote service for speech recognition or grading. Speaking practice uses your own review rather than an automated pronunciation score.

The app attempts to remove the current recording when you start a fresh attempt, leave the practice screen, or the practice is interrupted, including when the app goes into the background. A crash, unexpected termination, or file-cleanup failure can leave a recording in the cache. The operating system may clear cached files; the app's Data & Privacy erase control also includes the recording cache. Recordings are not included in learning-backup exports.

Learning backups you choose

You can export a learning-backup file and select a previously saved file to restore through the app's Data & Privacy controls. The backup includes supported local learning progress, reading positions, Dhikr counters, completion totals, saved and favorite readings, and Arabic course progress. It excludes raw microphone audio, app preferences, purchase entitlements, and credentials. Restoring a backup replaces the supported learning data after you confirm.

You choose where to save the exported file using the system file picker. A location or service you choose, such as iCloud Drive, may store or sync that file under its own terms. Ilmify does not automatically send backups to the developer or operate a cloud backup service. Erasing data inside Ilmify does not delete backup files you saved elsewhere; you can remove those copies through the file location or service you chose.

Widgets

Ilmify's Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets display a small selection of Qur'an excerpts bundled with the app. Their content is selected locally from the ayah and language you choose, or by a daily rotation. The widgets do not read your learning progress, microphone recordings, or completion totals, and do not send those data to the developer.

Optional purchases

Support offers repeatable one-time tips, auto-renewable subscriptions, and the one-time Founding Supporter purchase through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment. Ilmify receives product and verified transaction information to complete purchases, restore eligible purchases, and determine cosmetic access and subscription status. Ilmify does not receive your payment-card details.

Tips do not renew automatically or unlock additional content or app icons. Support subscriptions include the Blue and Rose app icons while the subscription is active. Founding Supporter is a non-consumable purchase for permanent access to the Amber and Silver app icons and does not renew automatically.

Learning remains free regardless of whether you contribute. Apple manages billing and subscription cancellation. Removing the app or erasing learning data does not cancel a subscription; use Apple's subscription settings to manage it.

Analytics and tracking

The current app does not include a developer-operated analytics service, analytics or advertising SDKs, or tracking across other companies' apps and websites. The local completion totals described above are not transmitted to the developer. Apple's App Store and TestFlight services process data under their own privacy terms.

TestFlight testing

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple collects and shares crash reports and usage information with the developer. Feedback you submit may include comments, screenshots, device information, and your name or email, depending on how you joined the beta and what you submit.

We use this information to investigate problems and improve Ilmify. We do not use it for advertising or share TestFlight testing information with third parties. Details about Apple's collection and retention are available in TestFlight & Privacy.

Messages, retention, and your choices

If you contact us, we receive your email address, any sender name supplied by your mail service, and anything you include in your message. Sending feedback is optional. Ilmify prepares a report for you to send through your chosen email or sharing app; it does not automatically send local progress or completion totals. We use correspondence to respond, provide support, and resolve the issue. Avoid sending sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

We retain support correspondence and any developer-held beta feedback only as long as needed to handle the request, investigate the issue, or meet applicable legal obligations. Apple controls retention within its own services.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information we hold, or object to its processing. Contact the email above to make a request. We may need information to verify that the request is yours. Requests about data held by Apple can also be made through Apple's Data and Privacy service.

External services and this website

Opening an external website or payment service from the app means that service's privacy policy applies. Email, Apple services, and website hosting may process information in countries other than your own.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts to this page. GitHub may process technical information when you visit it, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children and families

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of beta software and optional purchases. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through Ilmify. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when Ilmify's data practices change and revise the date above. New features that change how personal information is handled will be reflected in this policy.
