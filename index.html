Ilmify Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Overview

Ilmify is an Islamic learning app for iPhone and iPad with Prayer Studio, a Wudu guide, Arabic learning and speaking practice, Explore Islam lessons, a Qur’an reader with read-along, an ayah check, search by reciting and recitation playback, Dhikr & Awrad, My progress, widgets, a Community tab and optional purchases (Ilmify Plus and Support). Learning works without an Ilmify account, and Ilmify does not run its own cloud service for your progress. An optional account, through Sign in with Apple, is used only for groups in the Community tab (see Groups In The Community Tab). Optional Qur’an sync uses your own iCloud account (see Qur’an Sync With iCloud). Unless you turn it off, the app sends Ilmify anonymous daily activity totals that are not linked to you (see Anonymous Totals). Recitation audio is fetched from EveryAyah.com (see Recitation Playback And Downloads). An optional recitation donation, “Improve our system for everyone”, is off unless you turn it on; it is described in its own section.

Data Stored On Your Device

Ilmify stores information locally so you can continue between sessions. This can include:

  • onboarding completion, language and interface preferences;
  • prayer-reader and Wudu positions;
  • Arabic lesson progress, practice responses, self-review choices and listening and recording-practice metadata;
  • Explore Islam lesson progress;
  • Qur’an reading positions, bookmarks, coloured ayah highlights, personal notes and ayah folders;
  • Dhikr counters, saved and favourite readings, and reminder settings;
  • reader preferences, such as text size, translation and transliteration visibility, appearance and movement pace;
  • a history of your recent ayah checks (see Ayah Check);
  • the daily activity counts shown in My progress (see Anonymous Totals);
  • recitation audio files you downloaded or that are kept temporarily for playback, and your playback settings;
  • if you use Qur’an sync, its working files and a safety copy of your Qur’an data (see Qur’an Sync With iCloud);
  • explicit in-app completion totals used by My Activity, and prayer check-ins, achievements, personal goals and records;
  • if you use groups, your Community sign-in, kept in this device’s keychain and not synced, and the area you chose for Groups near you;
  • if you take part in recitation donation, the recordings kept for donating (see Improve Our System For Everyone); and
  • local purchase and entitlement information supplied through Apple services, and how many free ayah checks you used today.

Completion totals describe actions completed inside Ilmify. They do not establish whether a person performed worship outside the app. Answers to the first-launch questions about your background and interests are used to suggest a starting place and are not saved as a religious-affiliation profile.

The app uses local preferences and files in its private app storage. It does not upload your learning history to the developer. Apart from the anonymous daily totals described in Anonymous Totals, which you can turn off, the app sends Ilmify data only for features you choose to use: groups in the Community tab and the optional recitation donation, each described below. Apple-managed device backups are separate and depend on your device and backup settings.

Optional Microphone Practice

Microphone access is optional. Ilmify requests permission when you choose to record an Arabic speaking exercise, start Qur’an read-along, check your recitation of an ayah or search for an ayah by reciting, not simply when you open the app. You can decline permission or revoke it in your device’s Settings; features that need the microphone will then be unavailable.

Arabic speaking practice lets you listen to an example, record yourself and replay your recording for self-review. It records a temporary audio file in the app’s private on-device cache, with a maximum recording length of 60 seconds. These recordings are not uploaded or sent to a remote speech-recognition or pronunciation-grading service. Practice uses your own review rather than an automated pronunciation score.

The app attempts to delete the current recording when you start a fresh attempt, leave the practice screen or the practice is interrupted, including when the app goes into the background. A crash, unexpected termination or file-cleanup failure can leave a recording in the cache. The operating system may clear cached files; Ilmify’s all-learning-data erase control also includes the recording cache. Recordings are not included in Ilmify learning-backup exports.

Qur’an Read-Along And Local Session Records

Qur’an read-along processes microphone audio on your device with a recognition model supplied with the app, to follow your place in the text. Live audio and recognised text are used in memory during processing. The normal read-along feature does not save an audio recording or a raw transcript to disk, and does not send your recitation to the developer or a remote recognition service, unless you turn on the optional recitation donation (see Improve Our System For Everyone).

The app does retain local read-along session and diagnostic records. These can include a session identifier, dates and times, duration, the passage selected, surah, ayah and word positions, processing and stopping results, hashes of recognition output, model information, and technical audio-input, signal and route details. These records can indicate which passages you recited even though they contain no recording or raw transcript.

These records stay in the app’s local storage and are not automatically sent to the developer. They have no automatic age-based expiry in the current app. Ilmify’s all-learning-data erase control includes these records and the saved session reference. Session and diagnostic records are not included in Ilmify learning-backup exports.

Search by reciting listens while you recite a few words and then shows the closest ayat. The audio is processed in memory on your device by the same kind of on-device model; it is not saved, not kept as a search history and not sent anywhere.

Ayah Check

The ayah check listens while you recite one ayah, then shows words where your recitation may differ from the text. It uses the microphone only while you recite, for at most five minutes per check. The audio is kept in memory and checked on your device by a recognition model supplied with the app. It is not saved as a file, not sent to the developer or to any recognition service, and not used for the optional recitation donation.

So that you can see how earlier checks went, the app keeps a history on your device: which ayah you checked, when, how the check ended, and the kind and word position of each finding, including findings you marked as “This wasn’t a mistake”. It contains no audio and no transcript. It keeps at most the 10 most recent checks of each ayah and 1,000 checks in total; older entries are removed as new checks are added. The history is not sent to the developer, is not included in learning backups and is removed by the all-learning-data erase control.

The only information from the ayah check that leaves your device is the number of findings shown each day, as part of the anonymous totals described below, unless you turn those off.

Recitation Playback And Downloads

Ilmify can play recordings of Qur’an recitation by well-known reciters, ayah by ayah. These recordings are not hosted by Ilmify. The app fetches them directly from EveryAyah.com, a third-party website that publishes them. When you play a recitation, the app requests each ayah’s audio file from EveryAyah, including the next few ayat in advance so that playback continues smoothly. If you download a surah, the app requests all of its files and EveryAyah’s list of checksums used to verify them.

As with any website, EveryAyah’s servers receive your IP address, the time of each request and the files requested. The file names show the reciter and the surah and ayah being played or downloaded. The requests also carry standard technical details and an app label, “Ilmify (iOS app; Qur’an recitation playback)”. Ilmify does not send EveryAyah your name, an account, a device identifier or any other learning data. Ilmify does not receive EveryAyah’s server records and does not control how long EveryAyah keeps them; EveryAyah may process them in other countries.

You can turn off playback over mobile data in the playback options. Downloads use Wi-Fi only unless you change that setting. Recitations you have played are kept temporarily on your device (up to about 200 MB, oldest removed first). Downloaded surahs are kept until you delete them in Downloads. These files are excluded from device backups and from Ilmify learning backups. They contain only the published recitation, never your voice.

Playback does not use the microphone. When you start read-along or an ayah check, playback pauses so that the recording is not mixed with your own recitation.

Qur’an Sync With iCloud

Optional Qur’an sync keeps the following the same on your iPhone and iPad when they are signed in to the same iCloud account: your Qur’an highlights, bookmarks, notes and ayah folders, your reading position, and some reader display settings, such as appearance, font, page design and frame theme. Text size, layout and other settings that depend on the screen stay on each device. Memorize progress, read-along records, ayah-check history, My progress counts, microphone audio and all other learning data are not synced.

Sync uses Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit). It stores this data in the private database of your own iCloud account, where it counts toward your iCloud storage. The Ilmify app on your signed-in devices reads, writes and deletes this data only to keep your devices the same. Ilmify’s developer and its service providers cannot access it, and it is never sent to an Ilmify server or to any other third party. Apple stores it under the iCloud terms and Apple’s privacy policy. Your notes and the names and contents of your folders are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded; if you turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud, only your devices hold the keys to them. The other synced items (which ayat you highlighted or bookmarked, your reading position and reader settings) are protected by iCloud’s standard data protection. Sync status and errors are shown only on your device; Ilmify does not collect sync statistics or reports.

Sync is off until you turn it on in Data & Privacy (“Sync with iCloud”). When it first runs on a device, and again each time you turn it back on, the app saves a safety copy of your Qur’an marks, notes, synced reader settings and reading position as they were before the merge, on that device only. Safety copies are removed once they are older than 30 days (checked after each successful sync and when sync is turned off), and erasing all learning data in Data & Privacy removes them at once. Turning sync off stops syncing on that device; your data stays on the device and in iCloud.

Erasing all learning data in Ilmify erases this device only and turns sync off; it does not delete the copy in iCloud, so it cannot erase your other devices. To delete the synced copy, turn sync off on your devices and delete Ilmify’s data in your device’s iCloud storage settings (Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage). Restoring a learning backup while sync is on makes your Qur’an marks and notes on your other devices match the backup. If you sign out of iCloud or a different iCloud account signs in on the device, sync turns off on that device and Ilmify does not upload your data to the other account.

Anonymous Totals (My Progress)

My progress shows counts of your activity in Ilmify: time in the Qur’an tab (and, within it, time Recited aloud and time Read silently), ayat read, the number of Arabic letters in those ayat, ayah checks done and the findings they showed, read-along sessions started, time in the Learn tab, Learn lessons finished and reviewed, prayer lessons completed, Learn to Pray guides started, dhikr counted and shares. These counts are kept on your device and in learning backups you export, and the all-learning-data erase control removes them. They count actions in the app only. They do not measure worship or anything outside Ilmify.

To show totals for everyone who uses Ilmify, and to understand which features are used, the app sends Ilmify a short report. This is on by default, and you can turn it off at any time (see below). The app sends at most one new report a day. Each report contains:

  • the counts above for completed days that have not been sent yet (at most the last seven days, time in whole minutes, each count capped per day), with the date of each of those days;
  • for those days, how often each feature of the app was used (for example, opening the Qur’an search) and which QuranEnc translation books were opened;
  • a snapshot of which app settings are switched on, and the translation chosen to show inside the Qur’an reader;
  • the app version and a random report code.

The report code is made new for each report and is reused only when that same report is sent again after a failed attempt, so that it is counted once; it does not link reports to each other, to you or to your device. A report contains no name, account, Apple ID, device or advertising identifier, installation code, location, time of day, list of ayat, notes, search text, recording or any other text you wrote. It is never linked to your Community account. Only days from the day sharing starts on your device are sent; days while sharing was off, and counts that were erased or restored from a backup, are never sent. If a report cannot be delivered, the app tries the same report again later, for up to six days, and then drops it.

Reports go over an encrypted connection to a small service that Ilmify runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and a D1 database). The service checks each report and stores it as cleaned-up data, without your IP address or the request headers. Several times a day it adds the reports of each completed day to daily sums for all users together; at that point the settings snapshot and the chosen translation are removed from the stored reports. The stored reports and their codes are deleted after about 90 days (a scheduled clean-up deletes them once they are more than 90 days old and have been added to the sums, so deletion can take a little longer). The daily sums have no automatic expiry. Because reports are not linked to you, Ilmify cannot tell which report came from you. It therefore cannot show you, correct or delete your individual report. The totals are approximate: for example, a report that never arrives is not counted. The combined totals are shown in the app and may be shown on Ilmify’s website.

Cloudflare, Inc. runs the infrastructure for this service. To deliver reports it necessarily receives your IP address and technical connection details. Ilmify’s service does not store them and is set up without request logging. Cloudflare processes connection data as Ilmify’s service provider and, to operate and protect its network, under its own terms; what Cloudflare itself records and how long it keeps it are described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Cloudflare may process data in other countries, including the United States. While sharing is on, opening My progress also fetches the current totals from the same service; that request contains no counts.

Turning it off: switch off “Share anonymous totals” in Settings, in the Privacy section. The app then deletes any report that has not been sent yet and sends nothing more; a report that was already on its way at that moment may still arrive and be counted. Turning it off also stops the feature-use counts on your device. While sharing is off, the app also does not fetch everyone’s totals. If you turn it on again, only days from that day on are sent. Your own counts in My progress stay on your device either way.

Although Ilmify cannot link a report to you, we treat reports as personal data to be safe. The legal basis is Ilmify’s legitimate interest in understanding, in aggregate, how the app is used and in showing shared totals (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 31(1) FADP). You can object at any time by turning the switch off.

Groups In The Community Tab (Optional Account)

Groups are optional, in Ilmify versions that offer them. Everything else in Ilmify works without an account. To use groups, you sign in with Apple, confirm that you are 16 or older and agree that Ilmify may store your time with the Qur’an for the groups you join. Ilmify does not ask Apple for your name or email address.

What is stored on Ilmify’s server

  • Your account: a random account number; a scrambled form (a keyed hash) of the number Sign in with Apple gives Ilmify, never that number itself; the username and colour you choose; your “Show my time in groups” choice; the version of the terms you agreed to; when you created the account, when you last changed your username, when Apple last confirmed your sign-in, and the last day you used groups; and a random number for each of your devices (at most 5), made by the app and not an Apple or advertising identifier.
  • Your time with the Qur’an: for each day, two numbers counted on your device. Recited is time reciting aloud while read-along, Memorize mode or Mutqin follows your recitation. Read is time reading silently with a Qur’an page open on screen; nothing is counted while the device is idle or locked or Ilmify is in the background. The two numbers are never added into one score. They are sent only while you are signed in, “Show my time in groups” is on and you are in at least one group. No ayah, surah, page or time of day is sent, and each day counts for at most 6 hours per measure. For each group you currently belong to, a monthly aggregate also keeps your contribution from the day you joined during that joining month. It contains the same two time totals, not reading details, and is deleted when you leave the group, turn sharing off or delete your account.
  • Your groups: the groups you create or join, their names and invite codes, the settings their creator chooses (whether the group appears on the monthly board, its area and a monthly goal) and, in a shared khatmah, the juz you take or mark as read.
  • Reports and blocks you make, and short-lived counters used to limit misuse.
  • Account security: a digest of an Apple revocation notification to prevent duplicate processing, and a temporary revocation cutoff keyed by the same Apple-account hash already used for your account. These security records hold identifiers and timestamps, not Qur’an time, reading details or recordings.

Your prayer check-ins, achievements, personal goals and records, which ayat you read, and any recordings are never sent to this server. The anonymous totals report (see Anonymous Totals) is handled by a separate service and is never linked to your account.

Who sees what

  • Members of your groups see your username and colour, that you are a member and the day you joined, and, in a shared khatmah, which juz you took or read. No individual time row is displayed: a group shows only its members’ time together this month, Recited and Read side by side, and each total appears only once at least 3 members have that kind of time this month. Your time counts in a group from the day you joined it. When someone joins or leaves, the change in a total can still hint at that person’s time since they joined.
  • The monthly board lists only groups whose creator turned on “Show this group on the monthly board”, that have at least 3 members and at least 3 members with time this month. It shows each group’s name, number of members, area (if set) and time together this month. There is no individual time ranking. Group members are listed by username.
  • Groups near you: a group’s creator can give it a coarse area, either a country or a city of 300,000 people or more (city list from GeoNames). A group that appears on the monthly board and has an area can be found and joined by people who choose that area. Whoever joins this way becomes a member and sees what every member sees (usernames, colours, the day each member joined, the group’s totals); the group screen tells its members while the group can be found this way. The area you choose for yourself is kept on your device and sent only with a request for the groups there or for the monthly board filtered to that area; it is not stored with your account. The first suggestion is the country set in your device’s region settings, read on the device. Ilmify does not use GPS and does not ask for location permission.
  • Invitations: anyone with a group’s invite link or code can join it until its creator closes the group to new members. The invite web page shows no group details.
  • Reports: a reported name can be hidden. It is hidden automatically only after reports from people close to it (members of the group, or people who share a private group with that person) or from many people; other reports are reviewed by us. The reported person is not told who reported it.

Service provider and transfers

The groups server (ilmify-community.89tq2ph8mv.workers.dev) runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1), which hosts it for Ilmify as its service provider. Ilmify’s groups server is set up without request logging. Your IP address is used briefly in memory to limit misuse and is not stored by Ilmify; Cloudflare processes it as the host. Cloudflare may process data in other countries, including the United States. Apple provides Sign in with Apple under its own privacy policy.

Improve Our System For Everyone (Optional Recitation Donation)

“Improve our system for everyone” is an optional feature in Data & Privacy, in Ilmify versions that offer it. It is off by default. Donating needs an iCloud account. Ilmify collects nothing through it unless you open it, confirm that you are 16 or older and agree on its consent screen. Not taking part changes nothing else in Ilmify. If the consent terms change, recordings kept under earlier terms are not sent, and you are asked to agree again.

What is collected

  • Audio: recordings of the Qur’an recitations you make with the microphone in read-along (including Memorize mode) after agreeing, as 16 kHz mono audio. Recordings shorter than two seconds are not kept, and at most ten minutes of each session is kept.
  • Related details: the Qur’an ayah and page shown, the calendar day of the recording (not the time), its length, the app version, whether the app came from TestFlight or the App Store, the device type (iPhone or iPad), the major iOS version and the consent version.
  • A random donor code: created for this purpose. It is not your name, Apple ID, email address, contacts or location. Apple’s CloudKit also attaches its own pseudonymous, app-specific user reference to each upload. Ilmify cannot see your Apple ID from it and does not keep it when downloading recordings.

No transcript and no result of Ilmify’s on-device recitation check is sent. Recordings from the ayah check and from search by reciting are never donated.

Other voices nearby, such as family or children, can be recorded too, so donate when you recite alone. While a recitation is being kept for donating, the reader shows “Donating this recitation”.

A voice recording is personal data, and donated recordings are grouped under your donor code. We therefore treat them as linked to you, and Ilmify’s App Store privacy details say so. We do not use them to identify who is speaking.

How it is sent and stored

Recordings are sent only over Wi-Fi or Ethernet, never over mobile data, Personal Hotspot or a Low Data Mode connection, and only while you are signed in to iCloud. They go to Ilmify’s storage in the public database of Apple’s CloudKit, which Apple operates as Ilmify’s hosting provider. Only the Ilmify team can read them; CloudKit’s access rules stop other users from seeing or hearing them. Apple may process this data in other countries, including the United States.

A copy of each recording stays on your device until you delete it. These copies are kept in Ilmify’s private app storage with file protection, are excluded from device backups and are not included in Ilmify learning-backup exports. So that you can withdraw later, even after reinstalling the app, your donor code and a list of your uploads are also kept in your own private iCloud database, which Ilmify cannot read, and the donor code is kept in your device’s keychain, which iCloud Keychain syncs to your other devices if you use it. Both are deleted when you withdraw.

The app limits how much it collects: about 100 MB or 60 recordings a day, about 500 MB in total for each installation, and at most about 500 MB or 1,000 recordings kept on the device. When a limit is reached, the app stops keeping new recordings for donation and says so on the donation screen.

How it is used

Only to test and improve how Ilmify follows and checks recitation, for example how well it notices missed, wrong or extra words and vowel marks. This includes training and evaluating Ilmify’s own recognition models. Recordings are processed on computers controlled by Ilmify’s developer. They are not sold, not shared with other companies, not used for advertising or tracking, not sent to third-party AI or speech-recognition services, and not used to identify speakers.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Because a Qur’an recitation can reveal religious belief, it is explicit consent to the processing of sensitive data (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR; Art. 6(7) FADP). You can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before.

How long it is kept

We keep your recordings until you delete them or withdraw, or until they are no longer needed to improve Ilmify’s recitation checking, whichever comes first. We review whether they are still needed at least every 5 years. You can delete them at any time. When they are no longer needed or you ask for deletion, they are deleted from CloudKit and from Ilmify’s computers, including downloaded copies. Withdrawal notices (a donor code and recording names, no audio) are kept as long as Ilmify keeps any donated recordings, so that withdrawn recordings stay deleted, for example if an older copy is restored from a backup.

Age

Donating is only for people aged 16 or older. The consent screen asks you to confirm this, and nothing is sent without that confirmation. If we learn that someone younger donated, we delete those recordings. A parent or guardian can ask for this by email, with the donor code shown in the app.

Pausing, deleting and withdrawing

  • Pause: turn off donating on the donation screen. Nothing new is kept or sent until you turn it on again.
  • Delete a single recording: it is deleted from your device at once. If it was already sent, its copy is deleted from CloudKit the next time the app can reach iCloud, and it is never sent again.
  • “Withdraw and delete everything”: deletes all your donation recordings from your device at once and withdraws your consent. As soon as the device is online and signed in to the same iCloud account, the app deletes your recordings from CloudKit and leaves a withdrawal notice for Ilmify. Recordings made before you withdraw are never sent afterwards.
  • Erasing all learning data in Data & Privacy also withdraws the donation in the same way.
  • After deleting the app or on a new device: install Ilmify, sign in to the same iCloud account and tap “Withdraw earlier donations”.
  • Deletion request by email: the donation screen has “Email a deletion request”, which opens an email to Jason.omar.turkmani@gmail.com with your donor code filled in. You can also write yourself, with the donor code shown in the app after your first donation. Ilmify deletes every recording linked to that code, including downloaded copies, within 30 days and confirms by email. A parent or guardian can request this too.

Copies Ilmify has already downloaded are deleted at its next data update, within 30 days. This includes recordings sent from another iCloud account, which the app itself cannot delete. Models that were already trained are not retrained, but your recordings are left out of all further training and evaluation.

Learning Backups And Deletion

You can export a learning-backup file and choose a saved file to restore through Data & Privacy. Supported backup data includes learning progress, prayer and Wudu positions, Arabic course progress, the current Explore Islam course’s progress, Dhikr counters, completion totals, My progress counts, saved and favourite readings, Qur’an bookmarks, highlights, notes and folders, and saved Qur’an reader display preferences. Backups exclude microphone audio, read-along session and diagnostic records, ayah-check history, downloaded recitations, Qur’an sync files, purchase entitlements and credentials. Restoring a backup replaces the supported learning data after you confirm; with Qur’an sync on, your other devices then match the backup, as described in Qur’an Sync With iCloud.

You choose where to save an exported file using the system file picker. A service you choose, such as iCloud Drive, may store or sync that file under its own terms. Ilmify does not automatically send backup files to the developer or operate its own cloud backup service. Optional Qur’an sync uses your own iCloud account and is not a backup of all learning data.

The Data & Privacy erase control removes supported learning records, completion totals, My progress counts, ayah-check history, the Arabic recording cache, read-along session records, Qur’an sync files on the device including its safety copies, and any recordings kept for the optional recitation donation, and it withdraws that donation as described above. It also turns Qur’an sync off but does not delete the copy in your iCloud account; see Qur’an Sync With iCloud for how to delete it. Counts that were erased or restored are never sent as anonymous totals. Presentation preferences, the “Share anonymous totals” switch and downloaded recitations are managed separately. Erasing data in Ilmify does not delete backup files you saved elsewhere; remove those copies through the file location or service you chose. We cannot retrieve learning data that is stored only on your device, in your own iCloud account or in your own backup files.

Reminders And Widgets

Dhikr reminders are optional local notifications. Ilmify asks for notification permission when you enable a reminder. It does not send your reminder times to a developer-operated server or register a remote push token for these reminders. The optional daily reminder for the Qur’an or for learning is a local notification too. Its time and days stay on your device and are never sent. Its text can show the page where your Mushaf is open and your daily goal, and iOS shows it on the Lock Screen, where people near your device can see it.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show Qur’an verses, adhkār, hadith and short reminders bundled with the app. Which one a widget shows, and in which language, is chosen on your device from the widget’s settings and Ilmify’s language. The Continue Reading and Qur’an Lock Screen widgets also show your reading place (surah, ayah, page and juz), today’s Qur’an minutes and pages, and your daily amount if you set one. For this, Ilmify keeps these few values, and its language setting, on your device, in storage that only the app and its widgets can read (an App Group). They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. When you erase learning data in Data & Privacy, these shared values are cleared at once and Ilmify asks iOS to refresh the widgets; iOS decides when a widget on screen changes. A widget shows this information on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, where people near your device can see it, including while the device is locked. You choose which widgets to add and can remove them at any time in iOS. The widgets do not read microphone recordings and do not send your learning data to the developer.

Optional Purchases (Ilmify Plus And Support)

Ilmify Plus subscriptions (including Family Sharing where offered), Support subscriptions and tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. On your device, Ilmify receives product and verified transaction information from Apple to complete purchases, restore eligible purchases and determine purchase benefits or subscription status. This information is not sent to an Ilmify server, and payment-card details are never provided to Ilmify. Apple shares sales reports with the developer that do not identify you by name. Learning does not depend on contributing.

Apple manages billing and subscription cancellation. Removing Ilmify or erasing learning data does not cancel a subscription; use Apple’s subscription settings.

Analytics, Feedback And TestFlight

Ilmify does not use advertising services or third-party analytics tools, and it does not track users across other companies’ apps or websites. Its only usage measurement is the anonymous daily report described in Anonymous Totals, which you can turn off. Ilmify uses no advertising identifier and no crash-reporting or analytics SDK. Apart from that report, the Recited and Read time you choose to share with your groups and the optional recitation donation, local completion totals, ayah-check history and read-along diagnostics are not transmitted to the developer.

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares information with the developer. Feedback you submit may include comments, screenshots, device information and your name or email, depending on how you joined the beta and what you submit.

We use this information to investigate problems and improve Ilmify. We do not use it for advertising or share TestFlight testing information with third parties. Details about Apple's collection and retention are available in TestFlight & Privacy.

If you contact us or choose to send a report through your email or sharing app, we receive what you send, including your email address and any sender name your mail service supplies. The app does not automatically send that report. Include only the information needed for your request. We use correspondence to respond, provide support and investigate the issue.

We retain support correspondence and any developer-held beta feedback only as long as needed to handle the request, investigate the issue, or meet applicable legal obligations. Apple controls retention within its own services.

Religious Learning Content

Ilmify is a learning tool and does not provide personal religious rulings, live fatwa or emergency guidance. Source references are presented where the app provides them.

Read-along and the ayah check are automated. They can miss mistakes or flag correct recitation. They have not been reviewed by scholars, and they do not replace learning with a qualified teacher. Recitation audio comes from EveryAyah.com and is credited in the app to its reciters; Ilmify plays the published files without editing them.
